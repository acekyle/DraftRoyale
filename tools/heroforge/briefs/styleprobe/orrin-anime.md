He floats with open palms in relaxed A-pose, a thin flat ring of pale glowing dust orbiting beneath the hem. Colors: bone linen #c9c2ae, weathered taupe #5e5648, pale moonlight glow #e8f0ff. Anime action style: sharp stylized forms, clean cel-shaded look with PBR accents, dynamic heroic proportions, expressive silhouette. Game-ready, full body, single figure, strong black-profile silhouette.
